## Brand & Style

The visual narrative bridges Scandinavian functional elegance with editorial warmth, evoking calm intentionality, craft, and contemporary domestic serenity. Designed for modern home makers and design-conscious cooks, the interface emphasizes quiet luxury through breathing room, tactile tone balancing, and tactile physical metaphors without visual clutter.

The aesthetic fuses Japanese-Nordic (Japandi) minimalism with editorial boutique commerce. Key attributes:
- **Quiet Clarity:** Every element serves utility or creates psychological calm; decorative lines are kept to hairline weight.
- **Organic Restraint:** Earthy sage, oatmeal creams, and fired terracotta highlights replace aggressive conversion cues with warm hospitality.
- **Tactile Softness:** Micro-elevations mimic ceramic surfaces, linen mats, and matte-glazed kitchenware under natural light.

## Colors

The palette balances warm organic minerals with deep charcoal grounding to maintain accessible legibility across kitchen daylight conditions:

- **Primary (`#5E7A68`):** Deep Nordic Sage. Anchors core calls-to-action, selected tab states, and primary navigational moments. Provides reassuring natural balance.
- **Secondary (`#C86446`):** Warm Terracotta. Used strictly for seasonal highlights, alert tags, price incentives, and subtle editorial counters.
- **Tertiary (`#E4ECE6`):** Pale Mist Sage. Serves as container fills, active chip backgrounds, and soft badge backgrounds.
- **Neutrals & Surfaces:**
  - Base Background: Canvas `#FBF9F5` (Warm Alabaster).
  - Raised Cards / Floating Modals: Surface `#FFFDF9` (Crisp Oat Milk) and pure `#FFFFFF`.
  - Secondary Containers: `#F5EFEB` (Muted Sandstone).
  - Primary Text: `#222426` (Charcoal Slate) delivers optical softness compared to pure black while surpassing WCAG AAA contrast on cream bases.
  - Secondary Text: `#4C5055` (Muted Granite) for secondary metadata, specs, and form labels.

## Typography

The type system pairs the open, sculpted geometric friendliness of Plus Jakarta Sans for product headlines and collection titles with the precise metric rhythm of Inter for dense specification sheets, checkout items, and nutritional or dimension metadata.

- **Plus Jakarta Sans:** Set tightly with slight negative letter-spacing for headlines to create an upscale editorial magazine posture.
- **Inter:** Tuned for maximum optical legibility at small sizes, ensuring measurements, cart quantities, and ingredient guidelines read clearly at mobile arm-lengths.

## Layout & Spacing

A mobile-first framework anchored on a primary 390px viewport width, scaling up gracefully to tablet and desktop:

- **Mobile Viewport (320px–480px):** Single-column stack or 2-column product catalog with `1.25rem` screen edge margins and `1rem` column gutters. Horizontal carousels use edge bleed with `1.25rem` snapping offsets.
- **Tablet / Large Mobile (481px–768px):** Expands to a 4-to-6 column fluid grid with `1.5rem` gutters.
- **Desktop (769px+):** Constrained 12-column layout centered at a maximum canvas width of 1200px, utilizing `2rem` gutters and generous vertical sectional buffers (`space-2xl`).

Spacing follows an 8pt architectural rhythm, with `0.25rem` (4px) micro-steps reserved for compact badge padding, icon adjacency, and label pairings.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses warm ambient lighting rather than artificial gray drops. Surfaces emulate matte ceramic and unvarnished paper:

- **Level 0 (Flat Canvas):** `#FBF9F5` base background.
- **Level 1 (In-Page Cards & Trays):** `#FFFFFF` or `#FFFDF9` with a subtle outline (`1px solid rgba(34, 36, 38, 0.05)`) and an ultra-diffused shadow: `0px 4px 20px -2px rgba(94, 122, 104, 0.07)`. The sage tint softens the edge against the warm background.
- **Level 2 (Floating Action Bars & Drawers):** `0px 12px 32px -4px rgba(34, 36, 38, 0.08)`. Used for bottom checkout sheets, persistent bottom navigation tabs, and quick-add trays.
- **Level 3 (Overlays & Dialogs):** Backdrop blur of `16px` combined with a 40% translucent warm tone (`rgba(251, 249, 245, 0.75)`), creating an authentic architectural frosted-glass diffusion.

## Shapes

The geometric identity relies on rounded level 2 specifications:
- **Base UI Elements & Form Inputs:** `0.5rem` (8px) radius for precise, clean structural edges.
- **Card Containers & Product Tiles (`rounded-lg`):** `1rem` (16px) radius, conveying soft organic ceramics.
- **Modals, Bottom Drawers, & Hero Modules (`rounded-xl`):** `1.5rem` (24px) radius on top edges to produce comfortable tactile swipe surfaces.
- **Tags & Status Badges:** Fully circular pill shapes for contrast against rectangular product imagery.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action:** Solid `#5E7A68` background, `#FFFFFF` text, `0.5rem` radius, 48px height for thumb targets. Subtle spring animation on active depress (`scale(0.98)`).
- **Secondary Action:** Transparent base with a 1px border of `#5E7A68`, text in `#5E7A68`.
- **Terracotta Highlight:** `#C86446` background reserved solely for "Instant Purchase" or high-priority contextual conversion moments.

### Cards
- **Product Card:** Light background (`#FFFDF9`), `1rem` (16px) border radius, zero hard borders, supported by a 4:5 image ratio container. Includes an understated floating heart wishlist icon at the top right.
- **Curated Collection Tile:** Warm sandstone background (`#F5EFEB`), padded editorial text block, `1.5rem` border radius.

### Chips & Badges
- **Filter Chips:** Height of 36px, `0.5rem` radius, bordered with `rgba(34, 36, 38, 0.1)`. Selected state transitions to `#E4ECE6` background with `#5E7A68` text and icon indicator.
- **Editorial Badges:** Pill-shaped, small label typography (`label-sm`), featuring `#F9ECE6` background with `#C86446` text for discounts or `#E4ECE6` with `#5E7A68` text for sustainable materials.

### Form Inputs & Quantity Selectors
- **Input Fields:** 48px height, `#FFFFFF` background, `0.5rem` radius, `1px solid rgba(34, 36, 38, 0.12)`. Focus shifts border to `#5E7A68` with a soft sage glow ring (`0 0 0 3px rgba(94, 122, 104, 0.15)`).
- **Stepped Stepper (Quantity):** Pill-shaped pill counter in `#F5EFEB`, featuring tactile minus and plus icons with smooth feedback.

### Lists & Accordions
- **Technical Specification Lists:** Clean hairline dividers (`rgba(34, 36, 38, 0.08)`), left-aligned bold property in `#4C5055`, right-aligned value in `#222426`.
